Our Sponsors

Goderich Chess Club is a free, volunteer-run community initiative. The generosity of local sponsors helps cover equipment, tournament prizes, and hosting costs so admission stays free for everyone.

Sponsors

Goderich Public Library

Our founding host and biggest supporter - providing free meeting space every Tuesday evening at the Goderich Branch of the Huron County Library.

Goderich Legion

Our newest partnership - providing free meeting space every Friday evening at the Goderich Legion.

Rostra Marine Inc

We are a marine service company based in Southwestern Ontario with ties to local shipping, cargo handling, sail training, and tug operations. With a decade of shipboard experience and a diverse network of maritime professionals, we partner with companies and communities to promote a thriving marine industry across the Great Lakes.

Visit website →

Jokey North America Inc

For 55 years, the name Jokey has stood for outstanding know-how in plastic injection moulding and customer-oriented service. Our own tool manufacturing company, a division for technical plastic parts, and our high-quality bathroom furniture range round off our plastics competence.

Shoreline Welding and Machine Inc

Shoreline Welding and Machine Inc. is your one-stop shop for welding, machining, fabrication and repair work. We handle everything from custom fabrication of parts to large-scale production based work, and everything in between.

Knights of Columbus

The Goderich Columbus Centre has been an integral part of Goderich and the surrounding area for over 30 years. The Goderich Columbus Centre is where experience, professional service and small town atmosphere come together to make your meeting or special event happen!
