Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining the Goderich Chess Club.

Goderich Branch, Huron County Library, 52 Montreal St, Goderich, ON N7A 2G4 on Tuesdays and Goderich Legion, 56 Kingston St, Goderich, ON N7A 3K5 on Fridays.
Tuesday evenings from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM at the Goderich Library and Friday evenings from 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM at the Goderich Legion. Participants may come and go as they please during this timeframe.
No, this club is a non-profit club. Donations are accepted and used to directly fund: replacement of defective equipment, purchasing of new equipment, and a maintenance fee to keep the website hosted.
This club warmly welcomes all chess enthusiasts of any age to join us. Whether you are a seasoned player or a newcomer eager to learn the game, this is the perfect opportunity to immerse yourself in the world of chess.
No, there is no registration required.
You can be a beginner, intermediate, expert, or master level player. Players of all skill levels are welcome to join the club.
No rating is required to join the club.
Currently, the club normally has between 10 to 15 people that attend the club weekly on Tuesdays and Fridays. Our peak number of players that we've had at the club is 22.
Games are not recorded unless both participants agree to have the game recorded. Score sheets are not provided.
Yes, the club uses chess clocks. Players are not required to use one but are free to use one if they like. If needed, training can be provided to teach players how to use the chess clock correctly.
Players are free to play anyone they like at the club. No pairing software is used unless the game is to be rated.
Yes, spectators are allowed.
If the club is closed, there will be a post on the website, Facebook, and Instagram. Please refer to the posts / news web page, Facebook, and Instagram for the latest updates.
Drinks in covered containers (with lids) are allowed provided they are non-alcoholic. Snacks are fine to bring as long as it's not fast food. Note: to be confirmed with library staff on the official policy.
We plan on hosting rated tournaments in the future.
Yes, the club will host tournaments / events in the future. We have already held our first rated tournament and we plan on hosting more.
Yes, volunteers of any age are welcome. Yes, volunteer hours can be provided.

We'll reply within 7 days, and approved refunds are returned the same way you paid (cash, e-transfer, etc.) within 14 days.
